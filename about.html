Materials & care

What each piece is made of

Stone
Moissanite, a lab-grown silicon carbide stone, harder than any gem but diamond and with more fire. Colourless grades only (D–E). Carat weights are given as diamond-equivalent weight (DEW).
Metal
925 sterling silver throughout.
Rhodium
A bright, white platinum-group finish that protects silver from tarnish. 0.5 µm.
Platinum
A softer, cooler white finish. 0.5 µm.
Gold vermeil
18k gold over sterling silver, at least 2.5 µm thick.
Gold plating
A thinner gold finish for pieces that see less wear. Thickness stated per piece.
Care
Keep away from perfume, chlorine and salt water. Store pieces separately. Clean with a soft cloth. When a finish wears, we re-plate it.
